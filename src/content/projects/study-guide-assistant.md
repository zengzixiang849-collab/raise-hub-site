need: "Faculty want study guides and practice questions that stay true to approved course material. Students want help understanding and practising concepts from their own readings."

whatWasBuilt: "A CreateAI assistant that creates a study guide from one approved source, explains concepts using that material, supports practice with questions and hints, and acknowledges gaps. It was generalized into reusable instructions plus a course-specific Faculty Instruction File."
howTested: "Four question types (supported, coverage, paraphrase, boundary) run in Evaluation and read beyond the score, then a guide check for coverage and source support, then one correction and a retest of the same questions. In the student workshop, a recorded example scored perfectly but missed the required Chinese term translations. An exact instruction was added and the question retested."
limitations: "Retrieved material can support an answer but does not guarantee correctness. Reusable means adaptable, not universally validated. Score-based tests can miss instruction requirements unless those requirements are included in the evaluation criteria."
