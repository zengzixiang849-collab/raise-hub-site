keyIdeas:
  - "“Is AI allowed on this assignment?” is the wrong single question. Ask instead who should do this specific part, and why."
  - "Course rules come first. Then ask: can I verify it with a real check? If not, do it yourself. If yes, does this part teach me something?"
  - "Three boxes: human does it · human leads, AI assists · AI does it, human verifies."
  - "When AI does a part, brief it like a person: exact output, audience and tone, its part in the whole, and what it must not do."
practise: "A task map of a real assignment, built in pairs. Includes the riskiest sub-task and one part you chose to keep for yourself. Bilingual (English/Chinese) instructions."
