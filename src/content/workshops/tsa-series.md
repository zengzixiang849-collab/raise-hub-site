seriesPurpose: "Use AI responsibly to help faculty develop, test and maintain course-support tools using CreateAI, while keeping academic judgment and release decisions with faculty."
sessions:
  - number: 1
    title: "Know What to Hand AI"
    formats: [Observe, Define]
    keyIdeas:
      - "Faculty decide; TSAs prepare evidence. A draft, a saved project or a high score does not grant permission to release."
      - "Why we check: hallucination and sycophancy."
      - "Choose sources before trusting answers, and confirm the source was actually used correctly."
      - "Four questions make an instruction usable: job, source, boundary, next step."
      - "Apply the five RAISE dimensions to one answer, and note what that single answer cannot establish."
    leaveWith: "A completed Syllabot example and your own task brief for Session 2."
  - number: 2
    title: "Build an AI Course Helper"
    formats: [Build]
    keyIdeas:
      - "A deliberately bounded, fictional request: a one-page study guide and six practice questions from one approved source."
      - "System instructions persist; prompts ask. Knowledge supplies evidence; instructions define behaviour (retrieval-augmented generation)."
      - "Retrieved material can support an answer; it does not guarantee correctness."
      - "Turn a narrow build into a reusable pattern: stable instructions plus a course-specific Faculty Instruction File. “Reusable means adaptable, not universally validated.”"
    leaveWith: "A private draft bot, reusable instructions, a Faculty Instruction File and first-look notes for Session 3."
  - number: 3
    title: "Test It Before Faculty Use It"
    formats: [Test and revise]
    keyIdeas:
      - "Test, improve and recommend. Four test types (supported, coverage, paraphrase, boundary) plus two human reviews (impact, handoff)."
      - "Read responses beyond the Evaluation score: check every claim against the source."
      - "One observed weakness guides one correction. Keep the questions fixed, retest, and record before and after."
      - "The faculty hand-back gives evidence, limits and a recommendation. Faculty decide release."
    leaveWith: "Evaluation evidence, one correction with a retest, and a faculty hand-back recommendation."
