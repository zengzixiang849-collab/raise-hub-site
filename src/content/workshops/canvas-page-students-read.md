keyIdeas:
  - "AI predicts what sounds right, not what is true. Fluent ≠ correct."
  - "Use AI on text you already know and can check fully: reformat your own announcement, rubric or assignment. Do not use it to generate dates, facts or citations."
  - "Three guardrails: Canvas-safe HTML, accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA, Accessibility Checker and Ally), and practising on an unpublished page."
  - "One workflow: draft → check → publish. After every draft, either use it as-is or correct and recheck the whole page."
practise: "Convert one non-private announcement into a Canvas page with a starter prompt, verify every date and requirement, and preview it with the accessibility tools."
leaveWith: "One page you can post, and a method you can reuse every week."
